How the data was prepared

  • Only grades 1–6 are counted. Marks like np, nb, bz, T and lone +/− were removed, never turned into 0.
  • A corrected grade ([ old new ] in Librus) counts once, as the newest grade.
  • The export has no dates, so chart 3 shows the order grades were added, not real time.
  • Alert rules: fewer than 3 grades → INSUFFICIENT DATA; average below 3.0 → REVIEW AVERAGE; newer grades (later period, or newer half with 6+ grades) 0.5+ lower → RECENT DECLINE; 4+ grades and std above 1.5 → HIGH VARIABILITY.
No name, student ID or teacher comments are included on this page.
