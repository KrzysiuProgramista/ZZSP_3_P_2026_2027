Method and limitations

The analysis uses unweighted current grades on the 1–6 scale. It keeps only the later grade in each correction group and excludes status marks and plus/minus entries without an agreed numeric conversion.

The export does not give dates for regular grades, so grade counts are shown instead of a time trend. An average based on one or two grades can change quickly and should not be treated as an official result.
