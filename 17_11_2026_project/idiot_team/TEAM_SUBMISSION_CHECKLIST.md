# Team submission checklist

Team name:
Team members and roles:

## Round 1 — Data Integration
- [ ] Row counts recorded before/after every merge
- [ ] Join keys checked for uniqueness
- [ ] `validate=` used where appropriate
- [ ] Unmatched weather rows counted
- [ ] Unknown sensor IDs in incidents identified

## Round 2 — Traffic Hotspot
- [ ] District summary
- [ ] Road ranking
- [ ] Incident summary
- [ ] At least 4 evidence-backed answers

## Round 3 — Time Series
- [ ] Daily/hourly resampling
- [ ] Rolling statistic
- [ ] Period-over-period change
- [ ] Weekday/weekend comparison

## Round 4 — Traffic Mystery
- [ ] District and time window identified
- [ ] Fair baseline defined
- [ ] Roads ranked by speed drop
- [ ] Weather checked
- [ ] Incidents checked
- [ ] Hypothesis supported by 3 pieces of evidence
- [ ] Limitation stated

## Final briefing
- [ ] Three findings
- [ ] One summary table or chart
- [ ] Two recommendations
- [ ] One limitation
